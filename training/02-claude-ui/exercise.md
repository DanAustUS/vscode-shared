# Module 2 Exercise — Your First AI Instruction

## The Good Instruction Framework

Every strong Claude instruction has three parts. Miss one and you get a generic answer. Include all three and you get something you can actually use.

| Part | What it covers | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| **Who you are** | Your business context | "I run a small physiotherapy clinic with 3 staff..." |
| **What you need** | The specific task | "...write a reply to a patient who missed their appointment without cancelling..." |
| **The format** | How you want it back | "...in a warm but professional tone, under 100 words, ready to copy and paste." |

---

## Weak vs Strong — same task, very different results

**Weak instruction:**
> Write an email to a client who missed their appointment.

Claude will write something generic. You will spend 10 minutes editing it to fit your business.

**Strong instruction:**
> I run a small physiotherapy clinic. We had a patient miss their appointment yesterday with no notice. Write a short email (under 80 words) to send them. Warm tone — we want to keep the relationship. Include a link placeholder for rebooking and ask them to let us know if something came up.

Claude will write something you can send with one or two small edits.

## Put it together and paste it into claude.ai

Combine your three parts into one paragraph and paste it into a new conversation at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai).

See what comes back. If it is not quite right, tell Claude what to change:
- "Make it shorter"
- "Make the tone warmer"
- "Add a sentence about..."

That back-and-forth is normal. You are not doing it wrong. You are just having a conversation.
