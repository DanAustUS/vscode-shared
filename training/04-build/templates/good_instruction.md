# The Good Instruction Template

## For Claude at claude.ai (Module 2)

Three parts. All three, every time.

```
I [who you are and what your business does].

[The specific task you need — be as detailed as you can].

[The format you want back: length, tone, ready to send or draft, bullet points or paragraphs].
```

### Example

```
I run a small property management company with 12 rental properties. 
Most of my tenants pay on time but I have two who are consistently 5-7 days late.

Write a firm but professional email reminder to send to a late-paying tenant. 
Do not be aggressive — I want to keep the relationship. 
Mention that rent was due on the 1st and is now [X] days overdue. 
Include a line asking them to confirm they have transferred or let me know if there is an issue.

Keep it under 120 words. Professional tone. Ready to send — no placeholders.
```

## For Claude Code (Module 3 and 4)

Same three parts, but add a fourth for Claude Code: **what to create**.

```
I [who you are and what your business does].

The problem I want to solve: [the repetitive task or process you want to automate].

When it runs, it should: [what the agent does, step by step].

The result should: [where the output goes — terminal, file, email, etc.].

Please create [the files, scripts, and folders needed].
Keep the code simple. I am not a developer.
```

---

## The most common mistake

Being too vague.

**Too vague:** "Write me a summary of my emails."  
**Specific enough:** "Read the files in my notes/ folder. Find any line that contains URGENT or TODAY. Print those lines as a numbered list with today's date at the top."

The more specific you are, the less editing you have to do afterward.
